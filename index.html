Little Us Health

Little Us Health is a small private tool for one household of two people. It reads one person's own sleep, heart rate variability and resting heart rate from their Google Health account. It turns them into a daily freshness score: fresh, average, tired or stressed. The score appears inside Little Us, the household's private app.

It is not a public service. It has one user, who signs in with their own Google account, and it is not sold, advertised or offered to anyone else.

What it reads

Sleep sessions and stages, daily heart rate variability, resting heart rate, respiratory rate and skin temperature change, through the read-only Google Health API scopes for sleep and for health metrics and measurements.

Read the privacy policy for how the data is used, kept and deleted.
